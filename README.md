# TrueEarnings

**An AI first pass at quality of earnings for small-business sales.**

Live demo: **[open TrueEarnings](https://gmakusha.github.io/trueearnings/)** · Run the AI live: [Claude version](https://claude.ai/artifact/8mnVtrVb1diaPj2pMhEAKp)

Built by Givemore Makusha, The College of St. Scholastica (B.Sc. Finance, B.A. Mathematics), for the AINA Scholarship 2026–27.

## The problem

When a small business sells, the price is a multiple of its *adjusted* earnings: reported profit after removing the owner's personal costs, one-time events and items booked in the wrong year. Finding those adjustments is a quality-of-earnings (QoE) review. It typically costs $10,000–$30,000, and with the median small-business sale near $350,000, most deals skip it. At a ~2.5× multiple, every dollar of earnings that is missed or overstated moves the price by about $2.50.

## How it works

1. **Code does the arithmetic.** The general ledger is grouped by account and payee. For each group it computes the total, transaction count, months active, memo patterns and implied hourly rates.
2. **Claude does the judgment.** The grouped ledger and the reported P&L go to Claude with a QoE analyst brief. Claude proposes adjustments. Each one has a direction (add-back or deduction), an estimated amount, a defensibility rating, its reasoning, any market-rate assumption, the documents that would prove it, and a question for the owner.
3. **The analyst decides.** Each adjustment can be accepted or rejected. The EBITDA bridge, the value at a chosen multiple and a one-page hand-off summary update live. Every finding links back to the exact ledger lines behind it.

## The benchmark

The demo company, Birchline Landscape & Design, LLC, is fictional. Its FY2025 ledger has 945 transactions, generated by [`src/gen_ledger.py`](src/gen_ledger.py). Before any AI run, I planted **15 adjustments** and **13 decoys** (legitimate costs that look suspicious) and wrote the answer key in [`data/answer_key.json`](data/answer_key.json). The AI never sees the key.

| Method | Planted adjustments found (of 15) | Decoys wrongly flagged (of 13) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Keyword screen (settlement, personal, club, tuition, refund…) | 9, with no direction or amount | 3 |
| **TrueEarnings (Claude)** | **14 with the right direction and an amount; the 15th flagged at $0 pending market-rent data** | **0** |

The keyword screen catches items whose memos name the problem. The AI also caught the items that need reasoning: a spouse on payroll alongside a full-time office manager, the owner's son paid half the crew-lead rate, six months of missing workers' comp premiums, next year's insurance expensed this year, and a one-time storm contract inflating revenue.

The raw AI output is in [`data/ai_run_2026-10-31.json`](data/ai_run_2026-10-31.json). It's a single run of Claude (complex tier) that took 76 seconds.

## What it can't do yet

- **Income statement only.** There's no net working capital, proof of cash or review of debt-like items.
- **Flags are hypotheses.** Nothing should reach a buyer until the listed documents are reviewed.
- **Market rates are model estimates,** stated as assumptions. A real engagement uses local comps.
- **One synthetic company.** The benchmark shows the method works on known cases. It is not a statistical accuracy rate.
- **Privacy.** Uploaded ledgers are sent to Claude, so use anonymized data.

**Next steps:** three-year trend analysis, bank-statement proof of cash, direct QuickBooks/Xero import, a seller-readiness mode, and a buyer mode for testing a broker's add-back schedule.

## Repository

```
index.html                     the app (single file, no server)
data/ledger_fy2025.csv         the demo general ledger
data/answer_key.json           planted adjustments and decoys
data/ai_run_2026-10-31.json    the AI's raw findings
src/gen_ledger.py              generates the ledger and answer key
src/template.html, build.py    app source and build script
```

To rebuild: `python3 src/gen_ledger.py`, then `python3 src/build.py data/ai_run_2026-10-31.json standalone index.html` (run from a folder containing `template.html`).

*Birchline Landscape & Design and everyone in its ledger are fictional.*
